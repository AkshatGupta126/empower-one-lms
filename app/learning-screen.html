Cycle counting reference

This learning material supports consistent inventory control across stores and production locations.

Key practice points

  • Plan counting frequency according to inventory value and operational risk.
  • Record variances at the point of count and investigate root causes.
  • Keep stock movement controlled while a location is being verified.

Apply the approved site procedure and escalate discrepancies through the materials team.
