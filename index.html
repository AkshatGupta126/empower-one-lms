01
The Capability Gap

Machines are calibrated.
Processes are audited.
Capability is assumed.

Manufacturing has built genuine excellence in operating systems — TPM, TQM, lean, ISO, IATF. Workforce capability is the one input that never had an equivalent system.

Machines

Maintained on a schedule — calibration plans, maintenance cycles, uptime logs, service records.

Systematic

Processes

Checked by audit — SOPs, work instructions, documented controls, ISO compliance and certification.

Systematic

People

Trained when time allows — depth varies, records are scattered, and gaps are invisible.

Ad hoc
Not regular

A session runs when it can be scheduled — not when a person needs it.

Not standardised

The same role is taught differently across shifts, plants and trainers.

Not in one place

Records sit in registers, sheets and people's heads — no single view of who is trained.

03
Platform Capabilities

One platform.
Every part of the training workflow.

From content to completion — EmpowerOne gives HR and plant leaders a single system to assign, track and report on workforce training.

Learn

Structured course content

Courses organised into modules, with video learning, PDF documents and MCQ assessments — accessible from any device.

Assign

Role-based assignment

Courses are assigned by department and role. Every learner works through what is relevant to them — nothing more, nothing less.

Track

Progress & completion visibility

See where each learner stands. Resume learning picks up exactly where they left off.

Identify

Training gap visibility

Learners who are behind are visible at organisation and department level — so nothing slips through unnoticed.

Act

Follow-up workflow

Send reminders, reassign courses or extend deadlines directly from the dashboard. Action is one step from insight.

Report

HR & leadership reporting

Organisation and department-level training reports for HR teams and plant leaders — without manual collation.
