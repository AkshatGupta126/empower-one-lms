The Problem

Machines are calibrated. Processes are audited.
Capability is assumed.

Indian manufacturing has built genuine excellence in systems - TPM, TQM, lean, ISO and IATF. Workforce capability is the one input that never had an equivalent system.

⚙

Machines

Maintained on a schedule — calibration plans, maintenance cycles, uptime logs.

Systematic
📋

Processes

Checked by audit — SOPs, work instructions, certifications, ISO compliance.

Systematic
👷

People

Trained when time allows — sessions happen, but depth varies and records are scattered.

Ad hoc
⏰
Not regular

A session runs when it can be scheduled, not when a person needs it

🔄
Not standardised

The same role is taught differently across shifts, plants and trainers

📁
Not in one place

Records sit in registers, sheets and people's heads — no single view of who is trained
